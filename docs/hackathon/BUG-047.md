# BUG-047: Partial refunds rounded fee reversals independently

**Severity:** High  
**Component:** FP-300 refund accounting  
**Status:** Fixed and verified

## Evidence

Each refund previously reversed `round(capture fee × this refund / captured
amount)` independently. With a 1,000-fils capture and a 25-fils fee, two
500-fils refunds each rounded 12.5 fils to 12 under the finance rounding rule.
After refunding the entire capture, only 24 of the 25 fils had been reversed.

## Fix

The refund processor now computes the fee reversal owed on the cumulative
refunded amount, then subtracts the fee already reversed by successful prior
refunds. The 500 + 500 example allocates 12 + 13 fils, fully reversing the
original fee. Existing fee arithmetic remains in the frozen money package.

## Regression coverage

`processRefund.cumulativeFee.test.ts` creates and captures a real transaction,
performs both partial refunds through the refund processor, and verifies that
the refund rows reverse exactly the 25-fils capture fee. The focused test and
API TypeScript typecheck passed against the isolated audit database.
